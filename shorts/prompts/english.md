ENGLISH VIDEO: this video is narrated in English for an international audience. These rules replace the Tamil rules
above wherever they differ.

- Field `en` is the spoken narration: natural, warm spoken English, like a good presenter talking to one friend. At
  most 14 words per sentence, at most 2 sentences per line, plain everyday words. Write names and acronyms as people
  write them (the voice reads them). No symbols the voice cannot read (write "percent", "dollars", "kilometres").
  Mark exactly one key word per line with asterisks (it is highlighted on screen, never spoken).
- Field `ta` is the same line translated into simple Tamil (kept for the record; it is not spoken).
- Every on-screen word (hook, overlays, labels, graphic titles) is in English.
- title: English only, max 90 characters, no Tamil script, no " | ". description: 3 to 6 English lines.
  hashtags: 8 to 12 English hashtags for a worldwide audience: the 3 broadest that people search first, then
  specific ones, #knowthisdaily last; never #tamil. tags: 10 to 20 English keywords. en_title, en_description,
  en_hashtags: the same as title, description and hashtags.
