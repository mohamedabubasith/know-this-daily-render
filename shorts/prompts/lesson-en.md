ENGLISH LESSON: this lesson is narrated in English for an international audience. These rules replace the Tamil
rules above wherever they differ.

- Field `en` is the spoken narration: natural, warm spoken English, like a great teacher talking to one friend.
  20 to 30 lines, 300 to 420 words. At most 14 words per sentence, at most 2 sentences per line. Plain everyday
  words; explain every technical word the first time with a simple comparison. Write AWS, EC2, S3 and other names
  as people write them (the voice reads them). No symbols the voice cannot read (write "percent", "dollars").
  Mark exactly one key word per line with asterisks (it is highlighted on screen, never spoken).
- Field `ta` is the same line translated into simple Tamil (kept for the record; it is not spoken).
- The viewers are ordinary people anywhere in the world with no tech background. Every idea starts from a real-life
  example they live every day, then names the technical thing: renting a flat instead of building a house (the
  cloud), a storage locker you rent by size (S3), renting a car by the hour (EC2), a chain of warehouses in many
  cities (regions), an electricity meter that bills what you use (pay as you go). Keep the comparison running
  through the scene, and say plainly how the real thing is like it and where it differs.
- Comparisons and objects come from everyday life worldwide, not one country.
- Labels on the props are short English words.
- The last line gives the takeaway and asks viewers to subscribe to Know This Daily for the next lesson of the series.
- title: English only, max 90 characters, no Tamil script, no " | ". description: 4 to 8 English lines, ending with
  "Subscribe to Know This Daily for the next lesson." hashtags: 10 to 12 English hashtags for a worldwide
  audience: first the 3 broadest that people search (e.g. #aws #cloudcomputing #amazonwebservices), then specific
  and beginner ones (e.g. #awsforbeginners #learnaws #awstutorial #cloudcomputingforbeginners #techexplained), then
  #knowthisdaily last; never #tamil, never #shorts. tags: 10 to 20 English keywords. en_title,
  en_description, en_hashtags: the same as title, description and hashtags.
